Who We Are

Moscrop Robotics is a student-led competitive robotics program based at Moscrop Secondary School Robotics. Competing in the VEX Robotics Competition under team number 32987, our club brings together students who are passionate about engineering, programming, design, and teamwork. From brainstorming ideas and prototyping mechanisms to building, coding, and competing, every part of our robots is designed and created by students. Our current teams include 32987A and 32987B, with plans to expand to a third team in the future as our program continues to grow.

Beyond competition, Moscrop Robotics is about creating opportunities for students to learn practical STEM skills in a hands-on environment. Members gain experience in CAD, fabrication, programming, documentation, leadership, and collaboration while working through real engineering challenges together. We aim to build a team culture that values curiosity, creativity, and continuous improvement whether that means refining a drivetrain late after school or helping newer members learn the basics for the first time.

As our program grows, we are working to make robotics more accessible to students at our school and in the community. Through dedication, innovation, and teamwork, Moscrop Robotics is building more than robots. We are building a community of future engineers, problem-solvers, and leaders.
